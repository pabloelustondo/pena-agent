# PME LinkedIn Application Plan
## Strategic LinkedIn Opportunity Analysis and Application Workflow

Author: Pablo Elustondo  
Date: 2026-05-17

---

# Objective

Transform LinkedIn job discovery from random application behavior into a structured career intelligence and prioritization process.

The goal is not:
- maximum applications,
- blind quantity,
- or desperation-driven job hunting.

The goal is:
- strategic opportunity selection,
- market intelligence gathering,
- positioning refinement,
- and high-quality interview generation.

---

# Core Insight

The LinkedIn recommendation engine is acting as a market classifier.

The opportunities shown are signals about:
- how the market currently classifies my profile,
- what compensation band I belong to,
- what titles correlate with my experience,
- what technical domains match my background,
- and what trajectory the market predicts for me.

This makes the opportunity list valuable even before applying.

The 241 opportunities are therefore:
- not just jobs,
- but market data.

# Three-Pass Filtering Strategy

# Phase 1 — Rapid Classification

## Goal

Quickly analyze all LinkedIn opportunities at high level.

No deep reading yet.

Maximum:
- 1–2 minutes per opportunity.

Main task:
- classify,
- prioritize,
- discard noise.

---

## Output Categories

### A — High Priority

Strong alignment.
Worth deeper analysis and tailored application.

### B — Possible

Interesting but uncertain.
May revisit later.

### C — Low Value / Discard

Poor fit or low strategic value.

# The Three Most Important Scores

## 1. Technical Fit

Can I realistically perform well in this role?

Questions:
- Do I understand the stack?
- Does this match my architectural experience?
- Is the domain familiar?
- Can I speak credibly in interviews?

---

## 2. Identity Fit

Does this role align with who I want to become?

Critical long-term filter.

Danger:
Some roles may pay well but move me away from:
- technical depth,
- architecture,
- AI-era relevance,
- modernization work.

---

## 3. Market Value

Does this role improve:
- compensation,
- positioning,
- future opportunities,
- technical relevance,
- AI-era credibility,
- long-term career trajectory?

# Phase 3 — Application Execution

Therefore:

- prioritize quality,
- avoid mass-application fatigue.

Each application should ideally include:
- targeted resume version,
- LinkedIn alignment,
- role-specific positioning,
- concise recruiter narrative.
